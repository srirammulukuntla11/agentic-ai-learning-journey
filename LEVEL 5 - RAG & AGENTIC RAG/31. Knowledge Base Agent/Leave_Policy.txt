Company Leave Policy

Annual Leave:
Employees receive 20 days of annual leave every year.

Sick Leave:
Employees receive 10 days of sick leave every year.

Public Holidays:
Employees are entitled to all official public holidays observed by the company.

Leave Request:
Employees must submit a leave request to their manager before taking planned leave.

Emergency Leave:
In case of an emergency, employees should inform their manager as soon as possible.

Unused Leave:
Unused annual leave can be carried forward to the following year, subject to company rules.
