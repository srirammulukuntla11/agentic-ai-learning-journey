Company Work From Home Policy

Work From Home Eligibility:
Eligible employees can work from home up to 2 days per week.

Approval:
Employees must receive approval from their manager before working from home.

Office Attendance:
Employees are expected to work from the office on their scheduled office days.

Meetings:
Employees working from home must remain available for scheduled meetings and communication during working hours.

Internet Connection:
Employees working from home are responsible for maintaining a reliable internet connection.

Security:
Employees must follow company security policies when accessing company systems and data from home.
